## Runtime cell-interaction record (10 bytes)

Fields proven by `0x20C0C` / `0x20B40`:

- `+0x00`: type / zero terminator in per-cell lists
- `+0x01`: flags (`0x04` enabled, `0x40` activation lockout, low two bits transition mode)
- `+0x04`: cell Y (confirmed by `0x1E8C8` lookup and `0x20A24` party-Y comparison)
- `+0x05`: cell X (confirmed by column bucketing and `0x20A24` party-X comparison)
- `+0x06`: animation frame/state
- `+0x07`: signed frame delta (`-1`, `0`, `+1`)
- `+0x02/+0x03/+0x08/+0x09`: intentionally unnamed until independently proven
