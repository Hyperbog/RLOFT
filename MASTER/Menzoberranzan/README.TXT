Forgotten Realms: Menzoberranzan v1.1 Readme file CD Version 
 
The following problems are corrected in Menzoberranzan v1.1 
 
1) NPC Vonar can now scribe the light spell into his spellbook
2) Jump potions do not disappear when saving the game.
3) Malice will now appear in the endgame for everyone.
4) Game will no longer crash after returning necklace to Galeb Duhr. 

If you are using this update to correct problem #3, you will need to start 
at a savegame before ever entering House Do'urden in the city of
Menzoberranzan.

CLARIFICATIONS
1) In the disk version the game will sometimes exhibit a crash when prying 
gems out of the wall in the Dwarven Temple area.  Take only the white gem 
(diamond).  It is the only one necessary, and will not crash the game.

2) Many of the NPC's in the game do not appear in the disk version.  This was 
made clear in the documentation, however some users playing the disk version 
who call our 1-900-737-HINT line will hear references to NPC's that do not 
appear in that version.

3) If you experience a crash at any point during a cinematic sequence
(for example After returning the necklace to the Galeb Duhr), 
it can usually be avoided by simply hitting the <esc> (escape) key at the very 
beginning of the cinematic.  This aborts both the cinematic and stops the 
game from crashing. 

4) At some points in the game, those playing in `step mode' may find 
themselves unable to proceed down a passageway blocked with pillars,etc.  
Click the mouse on the compass in the main screen to get out of step mode 
and squeeze through the tight area in question.  This will occur 2-3 times 
for most users playing in step mode.

******** SPECIAL NOTE FOR SMARTDRV AND OTHER DISK CACHE USERS ***** 
 
SMARTDRV: 
 
SMARTDRV will not work properly with Forgotten Realms: Menzoberranzan 
in its normal configuration. 
 
You Can:  1) Remove SMARTDRV (you can do this by creating a boot disk) 
 
          2) Remove SMARTDRV's write cache by typing SMARTDRV followed 
             by the hard drive letter. 
 
             Example: SMARTDRV C  (Enter) 
 
             This will remove the write cache on your C: drive.  If 
             you have more than one hard drive, repeat this command 
             for each drive.  So, if you have a C: and a D:, you 
             would type: 
 
               SMARTDRV C  (Enter) 
               SMARTDRV D  (Enter) 
 
 
OTHER DISK CACHE PROGRAMS: 
 
Any program that has a write cache enabled is prone to data loss. 
Please remove the write cache or remove the disk cache program 
from your configuration (you can do this by creating a boot disk). 
 
 
If you do not take the above precautions, you may exibit data loss!!!

                            MANTLE CAVERNS: 
 
The two mantle caverns shown on the map on page 27 of the rule book 
are in the wrong order. 
 
Mantle Cavern One should be in the northeast while Mantle Cavern Two 
should be in the northwest.

                                POTIONS: 
 
Drink a Potion: 
 
To have a character drink a potion on the Adventure Screen, left-click 
on the in-hand potion. To have a character drink a potion on the 
Character Sheet, move the potion over the head of the mannequin figure 
and left-click to have the character drink the potion. 
 
Potion of Jump: 
 
When a character drinks a potion of jump, the ability "jump" is added 
to the character's abilities. To use the "jump" ability, left-click on 
a mage's golden spell book, a cleric's holy symbol, or a character's 
ability ribbon. To display the "jump" ability, left-click on the A 
which appears to the left of the 1st-level spell button. To activated 
the ability, left-click on the name of the ability. 
 
********************************************************************* 
 
                                SPELLS: 
 
If the party is half-way through a webbed door when the spell 
"passweb" wears off, the party can still step forward and out of the 
web in Step Movement. To enter Step Movement, move the cursor over the 
compass located above the movement arrows and left-click. Once in Step 
Movement, move the party forward one square and out of the web. 
 
********************************************************************* 
 
                          CHARACTER GENERATION: 
 
Character generation takes place in the chapel of House Baenre, where 
an altar is surrounded by eight burning braziers. One brazier at a 
time, the process of creating your characters begins. After each 
Player Character characteristic is chosen, the view circles around the 
scene to the next brazier. 
 
Select Sex: 
 
In the first brazier, two figures appear, one representing a male 
character and one representing a female character. Left-click on the 
appropriate figure to choose the sex of your character. Your selection 
is displayed near the bottom of the screen. 
 
Select Race: 
 
The scene circles to the next brazier and a candle melts to reveal six 
race icons. The choices are: human, gnome, dwarf, elf, half-elf, and 
halfling. Left-click on the appropriate icon to choose the race of 
your character. Your selection is displayed near the bottom of the 
screen. 
 
Select Alignment: 
 
In this brazier a tower representing the various alignments appears. 
Left-clicking over the choices displayed near the top and bottom of 
the tower causes the tower to rotate, displaying a new choice. The 
choices are: lawful good, lawful neutral, chaotic good, chaotic 
neutral, neutral good, and true neutral. Your choice may be limited by 
certain class restrictions. Your selection is displayed near the 
bottom of the screen. 
 
When the selections at the top and bottom of the tower show the 
desired alignment, move the cursor to the bottom center of the screen 
over the word SELECT. Left-click over SELECT to choose the alignment 
shown in the brazier. Your selection is displayed near the bottom of 
the screen. 
 
Select Face: 
 
In the next brazier, several of the portraits available to your 
character are displayed, along with the selection MORE at the top of 
the screen. Left-clicking on MORE displays more available portraits. 
Left-click on the appropriate portrait to choose the face of your 
character. Your selection is displayed near the bottom of the screen. 
 
Select Hit Points and Attributes: 
 
In the next brazier, the character's Hit Points appear, along with the 
various character attributes. 
 
Reroll: This choice randomly generates a new set of numbers for both 
Hit Points and Attributes. 
 
[Shift to MIN/MAX]: By pressing and holding the Shift key, then 
left-clicking over either your character's Hit Points number or the 
appropriate Attributes dice, the number of your choice automatically 
goes to its maximum possible value. By pressing and holding the Shift 
key, then right-clicking over either your character's Hit Points 
number or the appropriate Attributes dice, the number of your choice 
automatically goes to its minimum possible value. 
 
Attributes: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, 
and Charisma. To the right of each attribute three dice appear. By 
left-clicking over these dice the numerical value of the chosen 
attribute is adjusted upward. Should you wish to reduce a particular 
attribute number, see the instructions for [Shift to MIN/MAX] above. 
 
To choose the Hit Points and Attributes numbers appearing on the 
screen. left-click over the word SELECT centered near the bottom of 
the screen. 
 
Name: 
 
A tombstone appears upon which your character's name can be typed. 
Names may be up to twelve characters in length. Hit the Return-key 
when you have completed typing your character's name. 
 
If, when you press the Return-key, the character generator does not 
continue, you have either typed in an empty name or a name of one 
of the characters you will meet during your adventure.  Please 
re-type a new name for your character. 
 
Keep or Delete: 
 
At this point all the pertinent information about the character is 
known. Left-clicking on the word KEEP saves the character information 
generated and you are automatically returned to the first brazier to 
begin the process over again, this time in order to generate the 
second character necessary to start the game. Selecting DELETE returns 
you to the first stage to begin the generation again. 
 
Using the Escape Key: 
 
During the character generation process, pressing the Escape-key 
returns you to the character generation step previous to the one 
currently displayed. For example, pressing the Escape-key while 
choosing character Alignment returns you to the beginning of the 
sequence for selecting the character's Class. 
 
Pressing the Escape-key during the cinematic leading to a selection 
step will take you to the selection step.
